# Resume with the smallest sufficient update

Open the current project account and its decisive evidence. Check that the
checkpoint refers to the result you will use and that its conditions still
apply. Continue the next authorised action; use existing required records.

When a source, requirement or interpretation changes:

1. Identify the affected conclusion and dependent uses in the surface you own.
2. Inspect the changed evidence; reconcile its meaning, conditions and limits.
3. Retain the predecessor with a qualified pointer to its successor. Preserve
   useful negatives and unresolved disagreements.
4. Update the result, qualified checkpoint, responsible role and next action.
   Check the affected source links from the recipient's base.

Keep the current account short enough to resume, with necessary detail reached
through links. A changed hash/date, a hidden panel or shorter wording does not
establish a current conclusion. Defer the affected unsupported use while other
authorised work can continue. Coordinate concurrent edits using available
conflict/version controls and reconcile intervening changes before saving.

These are manual steps, not additional mandatory forms. Follow
[workspace guidance](kit/WORKSPACE.md) for a travelling minimum and
[compatibility](kit/COMPATIBILITY.md) for existing governed projects.
