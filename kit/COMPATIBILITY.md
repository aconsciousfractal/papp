# Use the contract the project actually has

For a new standalone project whose user chooses this guide, use its
[working agreement](CONTRACT.md). The guide's identity is
PAPP/documentary-1.0.3; its delivery is PAPP/github-1.0.3.
Choosing this agreement does not adopt a separate governing method.

For existing work, first inspect its actual instructions and method identity.
Where an adopted or explicit project contract applies, preserve its required
records, meanings, transitions and review requirements. This kit may provide
readable navigation and summaries. A replacement needs a separately scoped
decision; it cannot be inferred from a shorter note, finished task or new date.

Missing or unsupported identity in an already-governed subject remains a gap.
Do not classify it as historical or standalone just to avoid required records.
Resolve the identity-dependent use; continue unrelated authorised work.

## A dependency crosses the boundary

Suppose a documentary note locates a claim in a supplied scan. A mathematical
project wants to use that claim as an accepted premise. The locator proves
neither attribution to an authentic edition nor the premise's truth. Inspect
the proposition, domain, source status and support required by the consuming
project. If a decisive property is missing, defer that premise use and name it.
The note can still support the qualified statement that the supplied scan
contains those words.

Likewise, a narrative test report may be sufficient to resume a small software
task. It does not replace a formal certificate/checker manifest required by a
different consuming project. Preserve the relevant artifact and trusted basis.

## Changing or ending an agreement

Identify the subjects affected, required consumers, effective boundary,
authoritative homes and responsibility for the change. Retain the prior state
and describe how later use, migration or cessation works. Avoid a universal
extra form: an existing decision or handoff can carry this information.
Do not silently migrate old accounts or infer automatic fallback to a previous
contract. Reader access, evidence credit and authority for an external action
have their own requirements.

Already-permitted presentation and navigation require no replacement decision.
For example, improving a README's route to an unchanged qualified result does
not change that result's support or contract.
