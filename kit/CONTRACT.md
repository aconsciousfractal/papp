# Documentary working agreement

This guide is identified as **PAPP/documentary-1.0.3** and supplied in
**PAPP/github-1.0.3**. It describes the working agreement for a
user who chooses this standalone guide for work without a conflicting governing
contract. It grants no adoption of a separate governing method, conformance to
that method, replacement of an existing project contract or release authority.

## What must be recoverable

The [core](PAPP.md) defines the meanings to preserve: purpose and completion,
identified materials and applicability, actual work/checks, conclusion and
limits, and a qualified checkpoint/next action for durable work. They may live
in one existing native account; no universal JSON spine, extra ledger, fixed
folder count or set of empty files is required. A short answer can be inline.

The consumers are the person reopening the work, the next working agent and
any selected index/atlas. Preserve the information each actual dependent use
needs. Mathematical or scientific hypotheses, certificates/checkers, proof
artifacts and relevant review exposure remain required when the conclusion
depends on them. Missing decisive properties defer that use, not unrelated work.

Ordinary task progress and scientific support are distinct. Relevant contributor
and exposure information may be narrated proportionately; prose does not
manufacture independent review or satisfy another consumer's structured contract.

## Source and privacy boundaries

Describe copy-content, authenticated attribution and truth as different
propositions. A historical account does not rehabilitate its withdrawn scientific
assertion. This guide changes no adopted source-credit policy or privacy
schema. An internal note may truthfully record a privacy defect; an external
payload still needs its actual eligibility and action-specific authorisation.

## Change, compatibility and cessation

Existing governed subjects keep their contract and required records. A native
note may supplement them; substitution or migration needs its own explicit
decision, applicable scope and consumer behaviour. Do not infer a method change
from a missing version, a new chat or the word “experimental”.

Retain predecessor accounts under their actual contract, period and limits.
If use of this guide ends, identify the contract for subsequent work; do not
silently rewrite history, restore another profile or claim retroactive compliance.
For cross-contract consumption, use [compatibility](COMPATIBILITY.md).

No tool here automatically validates scientific meaning, reconciles changed
sources or produces a verified-current rich export. Optional atlas guidance is
manual. This agreement describes the chosen use of this documentary guide; it does not
establish measured ease of use or improved productivity.
