This example accompanies delivery `PAPP/github-1.0.3`, whose guide identity is `PAPP/documentary-1.0.3`. The original completed work used `PTP/native-preview-2`. The new packaging and labels are not a new use trial; its observation credit remains attached to the exact original notes.

[BEFORE](BEFORE.md) adapts the historical S1 note; [AFTER](AFTER.md) adapts the current S2 note. They are portable adaptations, not the exact historical files. Adaptations replace internal links and packaging/evaluation housekeeping; preserve the scientific account, qualifications and source locators. [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json) records original note hashes separately from the delivered adaptations and describes those changes. Human feedback concerns the exact original S1 note only.

The completed cycle was retrospective, nonblind and manually curated. The initial note reconstructed OPERA's superseded v1 report and exposed its result, decisive limit, evidence and next action. The human reader reported finding those four elements without help in that exact note. This is a qualified self-report of findability, not measured reading performance or acceptance of the later note.

After that response, a fresh-context assisted resume consulted the exact v4 full paper and reconciled the changed timing evidence. The current note replaces the anomalous physical interpretation with the corrected measurement compatible with zero within its quoted errors. It preserves S1 as historical evidence and retains the sign convention, distribution-based measurement, period/population, mean energy, effective baseline, calibration/waveform/distance assumptions and uncertainty limits. The selected event population and current source locators changed. The correction was interpreted manually; a changed hash did not perform reconciliation.

No physical replay, raw-data reproduction, independent physics validation, blind comparison, timed productivity study, installer trial or general daily-usability evidence was produced. Human acceptance of AFTER was not observed. Manual source checking and guide-reading overhead belong to this account.
